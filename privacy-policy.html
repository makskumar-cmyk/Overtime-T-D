What this app is

TX Calculator works out the loading on a three-phase distribution transformer from readings you take yourself: line and phase voltages, the current on each output, and the neutral current. It runs entirely on your device.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number, location or any device identifier, and it has no sign-in.

The only information it holds is what you type in yourself — meter readings, and the transformer name, reference number and notes you attach when you choose to save a reading.

Where that information is kept

Saved readings are written to the app's own private storage area on your device, which Android keeps isolated from other apps. Nothing is uploaded, and no copy is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Android backup

The app allows Android's standard backup service. If you have device backup switched on in your Android settings, your saved readings may be included in the backup that Android makes to your own Google account, along with the rest of your phone's app data. That backup belongs to you, is handled by Google under Google's terms, and is not visible to the developer. Turning off backup for this app in your Android settings stops it.

Permissions

TX Calculator requests no Android permissions at all. Its manifest declares no uses-permission entries — not even android.permission.INTERNET, so the app is technically unable to make a network connection.

Analytics, advertising and third-party code

There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. The app bundles no third-party service libraries; it uses only Google's standard AndroidX and Material user-interface components, which run locally and send nothing anywhere.

Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared, sold or transferred to any third party.

Deleting your data

You stay in control of everything the app holds:

  • Delete a single saved reading from the saved-transformers list inside the app.
  • Clear everything at once from Android Settings → Apps → TX Calculator → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored readings from the device.

There is no server-side copy, so there is nothing to request from the developer and nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

Children

TX Calculator is a working tool for electrical staff and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

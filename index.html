Privacy Policy

Overtime T&D · Android package mu.akshay.overtime
Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: Overtime T&D does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. Everything you enter stays in the app’s private storage on your own phone.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the Android application Overtime T&D (“the app”) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name mu.akshay.overtime.

2. Information the app collects

The developer collects nothing. No personal or sensitive user data is gathered, uploaded or shared. The app does not ask you to register, sign in or provide an email address, name, phone number or any identifier.

The app also does not collect or access:

  • Location data of any kind
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS or messages
  • Photos, media, files, or the device camera and microphone
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or crash and diagnostic telemetry
  • Installed-app inventories or usage analytics

3. Information you enter, and where it is kept

To do its job, the app saves the work details you type in. This information is written only to the app’s own private storage area on your device (its local web storage) and is never sent anywhere by the app:

  • Dates worked, and normal and overtime hours per day
  • Pay settings you configure, such as your rate and overtime multipliers
  • Vacation, leave and public-holiday entries and overrides
  • Calculated totals, including expected salary figures
  • A small preference flag recording whether the app’s intro has been shown today

Only apps signed with the same key can read this storage area. The developer has no access to it, and no server receives it.

4. No internet access

The app does not declare the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your data off the device or of contacting any server, advertising network or analytics service. Its screens are loaded from files packaged inside the app itself.

5. Permissions requested

None. The app requests no Android runtime permissions and no install-time permissions. It does declare a package-visibility query so that the sharing feature described below can see whether WhatsApp is installed; this only checks whether those apps are present and reads nothing from them.

6. Backups you choose to share

The app can write a backup of your entries to a JSON file and hand it to another app through the standard Android share sheet (WhatsApp, if installed, otherwise whichever app you pick). This happens only when you tap the share or backup action yourself.

Once you hand a backup file to another app, that file is under your control, and the receiving app’s own privacy policy and the recipient’s handling apply. The developer never receives a copy. Treat a backup like any other file containing your pay information, and share it only with people you trust.

7. Android system backup

The app allows Android’s built-in backup feature. If you have device backup switched on in your Android settings, your app data may be copied to your own Google account as part of the system backup, so that it can be restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. That transfer is performed by Android, not by the app, and is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can disable it in your device’s backup settings.

8. Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no third-party SDKs, no trackers, no social-media integrations and no payment processing. No data is sold or shared with third parties, because none is collected.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your entries remain on your device until you remove them. You can delete everything at any time by:

  • using the app’s own reset / clear-data action, or
  • opening Android Settings › Apps › Overtime T&D › Storage and tapping Clear data, or
  • uninstalling the app, which removes its private storage.

Backup files you have already exported and shared are not affected by this and must be deleted wherever you sent them. As the developer holds no copy of your data, there is no deletion request to send to us.

10. Children

The app is a work-time and pay calculator intended for working adults. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

11. Security

Your information never leaves the device by the app’s own action, and it is stored in the app’s sandboxed private storage, which Android isolates from other applications. Because the data lives on your phone, keeping the device locked with a PIN, pattern or biometric is the main protection, along with care over who you send backups to.

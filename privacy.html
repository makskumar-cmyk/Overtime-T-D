Privacy Policy

Overtime T&D — last updated 16 October 2026

Overtime T&D is a personal record keeper for overtime, leave and expected pay. This policy explains what the app does with the information you put into it. In short: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, upload or share any personal or usage information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services of any kind.

Information you enter, and where it is kept

The app stores what you type on the device itself, in the app's own private storage. That can include:

  • the days you have worked and the overtime recorded against them
  • vacation, sick leave, local leave and days off
  • public holiday marks
  • your company number (COY No.)
  • a monthly gross salary figure, if you choose to enter one
  • yearly leave opening balances, if you choose to enter them
  • any notes you add to a day

This information never leaves your device unless you deliberately export or share it yourself.

No internet access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission, and in fact requests no Android permissions at all. It is therefore not technically capable of sending your information anywhere.

Backups and sharing, which you control

The app can write a backup file containing your records. This only happens when you choose Export backup file or Share backup to WhatsApp, and you choose where the file goes or who receives it. Once you hand a backup to another app or person, this policy no longer governs it. The developer never receives a copy.

Your control over your information

  • Export writes everything to a file you keep.
  • Import restores from a file you choose.
  • Erase all data permanently removes everything the app holds.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes its stored information from the device.

Children

The app is intended for working adults keeping a record of their own employment hours. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Expected Salary is an estimate

Any pay figure the app shows is indicative only, calculated from the information you entered. It is not a payslip and is not issued by any employer. Refer to your payroll department for the exact amount.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this same address with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to the developer at the email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
